Literature & expectations

Authored by William Phinney · 21 October 2026

Each cloud of dots is one study, one dot per paper it covers. Hover a cloud for its citation and click to open it; hover a hypothesis to light up the studies it rests on.

The literature suggests that:

  1. H1

    Roughly half of retractions are due to misconduct.

    Sources → Fang et al. 2012 Grieneisen & Zhang 2012

  2. H2

    Misconduct retractions are more likely to have Chinese co-authors than the non-misconduct retractions.

    Sources → Candal-Pedreira et al. 2022 Mallapaty 2024

  3. H3

    Misconduct retractions take longer to be retracted than non-misconduct retractions.

    Sources → Fang et al. 2012 Trikalinos et al. 2008

  4. H4

    Older papers take longer to be retracted. (Likely, even once you adjust for truncation).

    Sources → Steen et al. 2013

  5. H5

    Author count has little effect on how long retraction takes.

    Sources → Trikalinos et al. 2008

  6. H6

    Papers of authors with many retractions take, on average, longer to be retracted.

    Sources → Steen et al. 2013
